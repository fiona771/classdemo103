About Us

OUR STORY

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who noticed many gamers lacked access to high-performance equipment and organised events. He created a modern venue where gamers of all ages enjoy the latest technology, compete in esports tournaments and socialise with friends.

VISION

To be Johannesburg's leading gaming and esports venue, where every gamer — casual or competitive — has access to world-class equipment and a vibrant community.

MISSION

To provide a modern, high-tech entertainment venue offering the latest gaming technology, organised tournaments and memorable experiences for individuals, schools and businesses.

GOALS

  • Grow the competitive esports scene in Johannesburg
  • Host tournaments for schools, gaming clubs and corporates
  • Make high-performance gaming accessible and affordable
  • Build a welcoming gaming community
